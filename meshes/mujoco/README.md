# Meshes of the MuJoCo TIAGo++ model

The meshes of MuJoCo Menagerie's TIAGo++ model
(https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/main/pal_tiago_dual) that the
other folders of `meshes` do not already hold: the omnidirectional base and its wheels,
the dual-arm torso's fixed part, and the gripper body. They are released under the
Apache-2.0 licence in `LICENSE`.

The MuJoCo model that uses them is part of `semantic_digital_twin` in
https://github.com/cram2/cognitive_robot_abstract_machine.
